# TOMLIN Sheets: what is new

Each version's lines are shown in the program's update box. Newest first.

## 1.0.0

- First release of TOMLIN Sheets for Windows 10 and 11.
- Opens and saves Excel workbooks (.xlsx), opens .xlsm (without macros), CSV and tab-separated files.
- 268 functions, named ranges, LET, tables, PivotTables, charts, conditional formatting, data validation, notes.
- Printing, page setup and PDF export.
- Two looks, under View: Modern (the ribbon) and Classic (a menu bar and two rows of small buttons).
- Recent files, one window per workbook, files dropped on the window open, and updates from within the program.
